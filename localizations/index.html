Games I wanted to understand.

Fan localization became one of the stranger ways I learned how games are put together: text formats, runtime constraints, patching, QA, proof and a lot of tiny decisions nobody sees.

The Dutch Xenosaga I+II DS build is the one I actually play and patch, so it now lives here as the primary Xenosaga release.
