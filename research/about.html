About / author note

I write the frameworks.
Then I ask how they could fail.

I am Raynor Eissens, an independent researcher working across AI systems, interfaces, institutional design and ambient computing. The research asks what changes as intelligence becomes abundant, and how those claims can be made inspectable, measurable and reversible.

Explore the full-text document library or browse all 202 source-linked records.

The through-line

From synthesis to test.

I publish conceptual papers, comparative frameworks, critical synthesis notes and empirical research programs. They do not all carry the same evidential status, and the site tries to make that boundary visible.

The recurring question is what remains scarce or difficult when machine execution becomes dramatically easier: attention, coherence, trust, continuity, power, exit and meaningful alternatives.

I want the record to remain inspectable. A DOI identifies a published record. An independent web article identifies a living synthesis. An empirical program should name measurements and falsification conditions rather than treating a future architecture as established fact.

Editorial note · added 19 September 2026

About this archive: evidence and publication status

Much of the theoretical work in this archive is exploratory and conceptual. Claims of novelty, priority or scientific status should be treated as provisional unless independently sourced. Publication records preserve the development of the ideas, but they do not constitute independent verification.

The collection preserves evolving hypotheses, speculative frameworks and other releases, including software. Theoretical claims should not be read as established scientific findings without independent evidence. A Zenodo record or DOI documents publication, not peer review, scientific endorsement or proof of novelty. The original records and extracted source texts are preserved without retrospective rewriting; this note provides context rather than revising the publications.

Methods & provenance

What these pages
do and do not claim.

A

Publication is a public record

These entries identify texts by title, source and DOI where supplied. A DOI does not itself confer peer review, independent confirmation or novelty. Full status must be checked in the respective record.

B

Conceptual ≠ empirical ≠ speculative

The research contains comparisons and proposals, possible measurements and speculative ontology. The site names those boundaries rather than presenting a single undifferentiated body of scientific fact.

C

Empirical programs are separate from conceptual claims

A proposed measurement, migration experiment or falsification criterion does not itself prove the broader framework. It defines what evidence would count for or against it.

D

Source preservation

The companion Conduits page explicitly distinguishes its newer thesis from the earlier Three-Axis DOI PDF. This website preserves that distinction, and uses the source's own nine-layer entry stack.

The archive preserves evolving hypotheses, source-linked publications and later critical revisions. Earlier records are not silently rewritten when later evidence narrows a claim.
