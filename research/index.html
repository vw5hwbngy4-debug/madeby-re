Independent research & building · 2026RE / 001

Intelligence is getting cheaper.
What happens around it?

I am Raynor Eissens. I publish conceptual and empirical research on the systems surrounding abundant AI: reversibility, attention, trust, distributed cognition, power architecture and ambient computing.

The recurring problem is not simply how much a machine can produce. It is what happens to attention, continuity, trust, power and the freedom to change course when execution becomes cheap.

05 / The connection

Artifacts enter the world.
Then the world changes what comes next.

One person can build a working artifact through iterative prompting. A public environment can give it a history, a context, an audience and the possibility of further variation. This is the practical bridge from the paper to the software.

make→publish→encounter→remix→preserve

An observed design direction, not a claim that a community or sustained retention has already been demonstrated.
