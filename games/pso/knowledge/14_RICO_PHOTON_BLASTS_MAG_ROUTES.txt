===============================================================================
1. HARD MAG MATH
===============================================================================

Mag level = DEF + POW + DEX + MIND.

Stat contribution to character:
- 1 DEF = +1 DFP
- 1 POW = +2 ATP
- 1 DEX = +0.5 ATA
- 1 MIND = +2 MST

A normal starting Mag is level 5 with 5 DEF.

Before RICO recommends ANY checkpoint:
- sum the four stats;
- confirm the sum equals the intended Mag level;
- evaluate the exact evolution formula arithmetically.

REGRESSION EXAMPLE:

Sato requirement for the relevant female Force / ID group:
DEF + DEX = POW + MIND

VALID:
5/50/45/0 at level 100
5 + 45 = 50 + 0
50 = 50

INVALID:
5/45/50/0 at level 100
5 + 50 = 55
45 + 0 = 45
55 != 45

RICO must never repeat the earlier invalid 5/45/50/0 Sato checkpoint.

===============================================================================
2. PHOTON BLAST CORE RULES
===============================================================================

A Mag can hold at most three unique PBs.

Normal PB acquisition opportunities:
- level 10
- level 35
- level 50

If an evolution would teach a PB already known, the slot is wasted at that
moment. Before a fourth-evolution form is locked, a later third-evolution
change can still teach a missing unique PB.

Once the Mag becomes a fourth-evolution Mag:
- it cannot evolve further from stats;
- it cannot learn another PB from later stat evolutions.

Therefore PB planning must be complete BEFORE locking Sato/Rati/Nidra/etc.
unless the desired PB set is already finished.

===============================================================================
3. THE SIX PHOTON BLASTS
===============================================================================

FARLLA
- nearby spinning area attack
- first PB from a Hunter-created Varuna

ESTLLA
- linear attack in front
- first PB from a Ranger-created Kalki
- widely valued as a flexible attack/chain PB

LEILLA
- heals participants in the PB chain
- first PB from a Force-created Vritra
- IMPORTANT CORRECTION: Leilla does NOT cast Anti or Reverser.
  Older community descriptions saying it does are incorrect.

GOLLA
- concentrated single-target attack

PILLA
- nearby aerial/AoE attack
- reverse-engineered client math maps Pilla to Golla's stronger damage
  calculation, making Pilla effectively a stronger general AoE choice than
  Farlla.

MYLLA & YOULLA (M&Y / TWINS)
- applies Shifta & Deband
- on initiation, also applies Anti to living chain participants or Reverser to
  dead chain participants; revived players do not receive the S&D part
- exceptionally valuable to classes that cannot cast Shifta/Deband themselves
- central multiplayer PB-chain utility

===============================================================================
4. VERIFIED LEVEL-10 ROUTING
===============================================================================

The class that performs the level-10 evolution determines the first form/PB:

Hunter feeder:
Mag -> Varuna -> Farlla

Ranger feeder:
Mag -> Kalki -> Estlla

Force feeder:
Mag -> Vritra -> Leilla

STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE:
If a build wants Estlla, the simplest route is to hand the baby Mag to a Ranger
for the feed that reaches level 10.

===============================================================================
5. VERIFIED LEVEL-35 ROUTING
===============================================================================

At level 35, the first-evolution family and dominant stat determine the form,
but the PB category is simple:

POW dominant -> Golla
DEX dominant -> Pilla
MIND dominant -> Mylla & Youlla

Examples:
Varuna -> Rudra/Golla, Marutah/Pilla, Vayu/M&Y
Kalki -> Surya/Golla, Mitra/Pilla, Tapas/M&Y
Vritra -> Sumba/Golla, Ashvinau/Pilla, Namuci/M&Y

For the common Estlla + Pilla opening:
- use Ranger at level 10 -> Kalki / Estlla
- keep DEX dominant at level 35 -> Mitra / Pilla

6. THIRD-EVOLUTION PB FORM LOOKUP
===============================================================================

From the packaged GC/Xbox-era standard Mag catalog:

- Estlla: Apsaras, Nandin, Durga, Soma, Andhaka, Bana
- Farlla: Ravana, Ribhava
- Golla: Varaha, Yaksa, Ushasu, Naraka, Kumara
- Mylla & Youlla: Ila, Kabanda, Madhu, Kaitabha, Naga
- Pilla: Kama, Bhirava, Garuda, Marica, Sita

Third evolutions begin at level 50 and can change again at every 5-level
checkpoint while the Mag remains eligible.

Useful M&Y handoff examples:

ILA / M&Y
- Hunter, Type-A IDs (Viridia/Skyly/Purplenum/Redria/Yellowboze)
- one relevant route: DEX > POW > MIND

KAITABHA / M&Y
- Ranger, Type-B IDs (Greenill/Bluefull/Pinkal/Oran/Whitill)
- relevant physical route: DEX >= POW > MIND
- another valid ordering exists with POW > MIND > DEX

These two handoffs make M&Y practical without permanently assigning MIND to a
physical build.

===============================================================================
7. PB CHAINING AND DONATION
===============================================================================

Players in the same room can strengthen PBs by chaining and donating PB gauge.

DONATION
- another player can donate up to 30 PB
- typically 10 PB per input, up to three inputs
- donation strengthens the chain and extends applicable effects to donor

CHAINING
- multiple players activate PBs during the chain window
- the same PB must NOT be used twice consecutively
- if duplicated back-to-back, an earlier matching PB can be overwritten and
  that participant loses the intended chain benefit

M&Y SCALE
M&Y S&D level:
1 + (PB amount * number of PBs * 0.01) * (Mag IQ * 0.1)

At IQ 200 and a normal 100-PB initiator:
- solo M&Y: level 21 S&D
- 2-PB chain: level 41
- 3-PB chain: level 61
- 4-PB chain: level 81

This is why community players place enormous strategic value on M&Y even when a
character can cast ordinary support Techniques.

===============================================================================
8. COMMUNITY CONSENSUS: PB PRIORITY
===============================================================================

This is STRATEGY, not a hard game rule.

A long-running GC/Xbox community consensus strongly favors:

ESTLLA + PILLA + MYLLA & YOULLA

for many general-purpose physical Mags.

WHY:

M&Y
- usually the most strategically valuable PB
- especially important for HUcast, HUcaseal, RAcast, RAcaseal, and HUmar,
  because they cannot provide equivalent self-cast Shifta/Deband
- extremely powerful in multiplayer PB chains

Pilla
- strong nearby AoE
- mechanically uses a high-damage calculation
- generally preferred over Farlla when choosing one broad attack PB

Estlla
- different geometry from Pilla
- easy to secure at level 10 with a Ranger handoff
- useful for PB-chain diversity because duplicate consecutive PBs are bad

Lower-priority general-purpose choices:
- Golla: useful focused single target but less broadly flexible
- Farlla: usable AoE but Pilla is usually the stronger AoE option
- Leilla: healing utility, but especially redundant for Technique-capable
  characters; it does not Anti/Reverser despite old community myths

RICO should call E/P/M a COMMUNITY META / DEFAULT, not a universal law.

===============================================================================
9. COMMUNITY CONSENSUS: STAT SPREADS
===============================================================================

The common physical baseline:
5 DEF / high POW / enough DEX / 0 MIND

5/145/50/0 is a famous, broadly useful console-era physical Mag spread and
appears repeatedly in long-running GC community plans.

But it is NOT universally optimal.

Why DEF is often kept at 5:
- each MAG DEF level gives only 1 DFP
- each POW gives 2 ATP
- each DEX gives 0.5 ATA
- community min-max plans usually prefer meeting defensive needs elsewhere

DEX should be planned around:
- class base/max ATA
- character level
- hit% on weapons
- /Arm units or other equipment
- material plan
- whether the Mag is for leveling or final Lv200 optimization

Examples of community physical targets:
- 5/145/50/0: common power-first general build
- 5/125/70/0: common accuracy-heavier middle ground
- HUcast discussions also cite spreads around 5/129/66/0 when matching a
  specific endgame max-ATA plan

Do not declare one of these "best" until class/build/material/equipment context
is known.

===============================================================================
10. CLASS-SPECIFIC COMMUNITY STRATEGY
===============================================================================

HUCAST / HUCASEAL
- MIND is normally wasted.
- M&Y is extremely valuable because Casts cannot cast Techniques.
- E/P/M is a strong general PB default.
- Determine DEX from ATA needs; do not blindly force 50 or 70.
- Online, ignore legacy Mag death-Reverser as a benefit; compare invulnerability
  and support triggers instead.

HUMAR
- cannot cast Shifta/Deband in Episode I & II.
- M&Y is therefore highly valuable for solo/general play.
- physical POW/DEX Mag is standard.

HUNEWEARL
- can cast support Techniques.
- M&Y is less mandatory solo, but remains excellent for high-level multiplayer
  chains.
- build can prioritize aesthetics/passives more freely once stats are covered.

RACAST / RACASEAL
- no Techniques, so M&Y remains premium utility.
- Rangers generally need less Mag DEX than low-ATA Hunters once mature, but
  early leveling may benefit from extra DEX.

RAMAR / RAMARL
- have their own support Techniques, so M&Y is less essential for solo than on
  Casts/HUmar.
- it remains valuable for PB chains and can exceed normal support levels.
- tune DEX against actual ATA plan; old community plans often use less than
  50 DEX at max level when equipment/materials already cover ATA.

FORCES
- high MIND is usually the stat goal for technique-focused play.
- they can self-cast healing/support, so Leilla and solo M&Y utility are less
  critical.
- M&Y remains valuable in multiplayer chains because PB-chain S&D can greatly
  exceed ordinary Technique level caps.
- do not sacrifice a desired final stat plan solely to copy a physical-class
  PB route.

===============================================================================
11. RARE-MAG PASSIVE PACKAGE VS PB PACKAGE
===============================================================================

PBs and automatic triggers are separate systems.

For ONLINE GC/Xbox play:

SATO / NIDRA
- choose primarily for automatic invulnerability behavior plus appearance and
  feeding/stat route
- do NOT count death revival online
- Nidra is mechanically more reliable than Sato on the three online-relevant
  invulnerability events:
  Nidra = 50% PB, 50% low HP, 50-85% boss
  Sato = 0-35% PB, 50% low HP, 50% boss

RATI / DEVA / DIWARI / PUSHAN / RUKMIN / SAVITRI
- support-style automatic package:
  S&D at full PB, Resta low HP, S&D boss
- remember automatic S&D caps around level 6 at IQ 200
- if the character already intends to fire M&Y at 100 PB, an automatic S&D
  trigger at the same event can be strategically redundant

BHIMA
- hybrid: S&D at full PB; invulnerability low HP/boss

COMMUNITY TAKE
This is why some experienced players prefer Sato/Nidra-style invulnerability
Mags over support-trigger Mags for physical characters, while others pick Rati,
Diwari, etc. for appearance/feeding convenience/support behavior.

RICO should explain the tradeoff, not declare aesthetics irrelevant.

12. VERIFIED SATO PHYSICAL ROUTE SKELETON
===============================================================================

Goal example:
final Sato 5/145/50/0 with Estlla / Pilla / Mylla & Youlla.

This is a ROUTE SKELETON. Exact feed counts must come from #11/current progress
bars, not invented from integer levels.

STEP A - ESTLLA
- Have a Ranger perform the level-10 evolution.
- Result: Kalki / Estlla.

STEP B - PILLA
- Keep DEX the dominant stat at level 35.
- Kalki -> Mitra / Pilla.

STEP C - MYLLA & YOULLA
Use a valid level-50 third-evolution handoff, for example:
- Ranger with Greenill/Bluefull/Pinkal/Oran/Whitill and DEX >= POW > MIND
  -> Kaitabha / M&Y
OR
- Hunter with Viridia/Skyly/Purplenum/Redria/Yellowboze and DEX > POW > MIND
  -> Ila / M&Y

STEP D - PREPARE SATO EQUATION
At level 100 Sato requires:
- female Force
- Viridia / Bluefull / Redria / Whitill
- DEF + DEX = POW + MIND

A valid physical checkpoint:
5 DEF / 50 POW / 45 DEX / 0 MIND
5 + 45 = 50 + 0 = 50

Give the feed that triggers the fourth evolution to the qualifying female Force.

STEP E - AFTER SATO
Once Sato exists, it is permanently a fourth-evolution Sato.
It will NOT re-check into another stat-evolution form at 110/120/etc.

To finish 5/145/50/0 from 5/50/45/0:
- add 95 POW levels
- add 5 DEX levels
- preserve DEF 5 / MIND 0

The exact order/items depend on Sato's feeding table and current progress bars.

15. FEEDER HANDOFF RULES
===============================================================================

A handoff matters at the FEED that causes an evolution.

RICO should describe handoffs explicitly:

"Raise to just below the checkpoint on Character A.
Transfer the Mag.
Character B performs the feed that causes Lv10/Lv35/Lv50/Lv100."

Do not vaguely say "use a Force around level 100."

At every handoff record:
- current Mag form
- exact level and stat totals
- known PBs
- target PB/form
- feeder class
- feeder gender when fourth evolution requires it
- feeder Section ID
- equation
- expected result

19. REGRESSION TESTS
===============================================================================

Q: I want Sato 5/145/50/0. What is the correct Lv100 physical checkpoint?
A: 5/50/45/0 is one valid Sato checkpoint for the female Force
   Viridia/Bluefull/Redria/Whitill formula.
   5/45/50/0 is NOT valid for Sato.

Q: Can I lose Sato at Lv110 if my Hunter feeds it?
A: No. Once fourth-evolution Sato exists, it cannot stat-evolve again.

Q: What PB set is the common physical meta?
A: Estlla / Pilla / Mylla & Youlla, labeled community consensus, not universal
   law.

Q: Why M&Y on HUcast?
A: HUcast cannot cast Techniques; M&Y supplies strong S&D and has exceptional
   PB-chain value.

Q: Does Leilla revive dead players?
A: No. Leilla heals. The old "Leilla casts Anti/Reverser" belief is incorrect.
   M&Y can apply Anti/Reverser to PB-chain participants on initiation.

Q: Is Pilla basically redundant with Farlla?
A: Both are nearby attacks, but Pilla uses the stronger Golla-like damage
   calculation and is generally the stronger AoE choice.

Q: Is automatic S&D on Rati as strong as Mylla & Youlla?
A: No. Auto S&D scales with IQ to roughly level 6 max; a solo max-IQ M&Y can
   reach level 21 and chains can reach much higher.

Q: Should an Xbox Sylverant player choose Sato because it revives on death?
A: No. Vanilla Mag death-triggering is offline-only. Judge Sato online by its
   invulnerability triggers, stats, PBs and aesthetics.
