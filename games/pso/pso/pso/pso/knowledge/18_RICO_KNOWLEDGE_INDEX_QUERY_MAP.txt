NORMALIZATION HINTS
- Preserve capitalization exactly for Section Scan calculations.
- Treat apostrophe variants and capitalization as search-normalization issues for item lookup, not different items, unless source semantics show otherwise.
- Some imported item names have spelling/case duplicates such as Df Field / DF Field or Handgun:Guld / Handgun: Guld. Preserve source labels when quoting a row, but consolidate conceptually when answering.
- Enemy/source naming may vary slightly across imported datasets, e.g. Dubchic/Dubchich. Keep the exact row label attached to its source.
