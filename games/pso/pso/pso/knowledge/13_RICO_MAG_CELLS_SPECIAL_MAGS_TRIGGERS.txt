1. CRITICAL MODE FIREWALL: REVIVAL ON DEATH
===============================================================================

HARD RULE:

On vanilla PSO behavior, a Mag's DEATH / REVERSER trigger is an OFFLINE-mode
mechanic.

For GameCube/Dreamcast-style offline single-player play, some Mags can attempt
Reverser when the player dies.

For ONLINE play, including an unmodified Xbox/GC client connected to a private
server, do NOT promise automatic Mag revival from the normal death trigger.

Blue Burst has no offline mode, so the normal Mag death trigger is disabled
there as well.

Therefore:

- "Sato can revive me while I play offline GameCube/Xbox" -> potentially YES,
  if Sato's death reaction is Reverser and the trigger succeeds.
- "Sato will revive me online on Xbox Sylverant" -> NO under vanilla online
  trigger behavior. Do not sell Sato's death reaction as an online benefit.
- A server/client patch may intentionally alter this. If a named private server
  claims a Mag-Reverser patch, treat that as server/client-specific live info.

This corrects older shorthand tables that simply list "Death = Reverser"
without stating that vanilla death triggering is offline-only.

===============================================================================
2. THE FOUR AUTOMATIC MAG TRIGGER EVENTS
===============================================================================

100 PB
- Checked when the player's Photon Blast gauge becomes full.

10% HP
- The player must lose more than 20% HP and fall below 10% HP in one frame.
- Merely standing below 10% HP is not enough.

BOSS ROOM
- Checked when entering a boss room.

DEATH
- Checked when the player dies.
- Vanilla trigger is offline-only as described above.

If multiple conditions happen in the same frame, the engine checks only one:

1. 100 PB
2. boss-room entry
3. death
4. 10% HP

A higher-priority trigger can therefore suppress a lower-priority check even if
the higher-priority trigger fails to activate.

===============================================================================
3. AUTOMATIC TRIGGER EFFECTS
===============================================================================

INVULNERABILITY
- Grants about 30 seconds of invulnerability from direct damage.
- HP/TP self-cost effects such as Berserk/Spirit still apply.
- Dark Falz soul-bind damage is a special exception.

RESTA
- Automatic heal.
- Technique level = floor(IQ / 40) + 1.
- Maximum at IQ 200 = level 6.

SHIFTA & DEBAND (S&D)
- Automatic attack/defense support.
- Technique level = floor(IQ / 40) + 1.
- Maximum at IQ 200 = level 6.

REVERSER
- Attempts to revive the player on the death trigger.
- Vanilla death trigger is offline-only.

IMPORTANT:
Automatic Mag S&D is NOT the same as Mylla & Youlla.
At IQ 200, automatic S&D tops out around level 6, while a normal solo
Mylla & Youlla at 100 PB can reach level 21 and PB chains can go far higher.

===============================================================================
4. SYNCHRO AND TRIGGER PROBABILITY
===============================================================================

Some trigger rates depend on Mag Synchro.

Synchro -> rate boost:
- 0-30%: +0%
- 31-60%: +15%
- 61-80%: +25%
- 81-100%: +30%
- 101-120%: +35%

Depending on the Mag/trigger, this Synchro component can be:
- added to a base trigger rate, or
- used by itself.

Do not convert a Mag's single packaged "activationChance" field into a flat
probability for every trigger. Different events can use different modifiers.

6. BHIMA / RUKMIN DEATH-REACTION CONFLICT
===============================================================================

Do not hide this conflict.

A historical GameCube/Xbox-specific Special Mag Evolution Guide lists:
- Bhima: death -> nothing
- Rukmin: death -> nothing

Ragol.co.uk's structured Mag guide and RICO's packaged Magatama-derived data
list Reverser for both.

Because:
1. the sources disagree, and
2. normal death-triggering is offline-only anyway,

RICO must answer an OFFLINE Bhima/Rukmin revival question as:
"secondary sources conflict; I would not promise the death revival without a
client/binary test or stronger platform-specific evidence."

For ONLINE Xbox/GC, the conflict is practically irrelevant: vanilla online
death triggering is disabled.

7. SATO VS NIDRA VS SUPPORT-TYPE FOURTH EVOLUTIONS
===============================================================================

SATO
- Triple invulnerability effects.
- Strong defensive identity.
- 100-PB invulnerability is less reliable than Nidra's because Sato uses the
  Synchro-only 0-35% component there.
- Online: no normal death revival.

NIDRA
- Triple invulnerability effects.
- 100 PB: fixed 50%.
- low HP: fixed 50%.
- boss: 50-85%.
- Mechanically more reliable than Sato for the three ONLINE-relevant automatic
  invulnerability checks.

DEVA / DIWARI / PUSHAN / RATI / RUKMIN / SAVITRI
- S&D at full PB.
- Resta at low HP.
- S&D entering boss.
- These are support-oriented trigger packages.
- At IQ 200 their automatic S&D is only about level 6, so compare that against
  the player's own Techniques and/or Mylla & Youlla before calling it "best."

BHIMA
- Hybrid package: S&D at PB full, invulnerability low HP and boss.

STRATEGY RULE
Do not choose a Mag solely from appearance or stats. Compare:
- final stat spread
- desired PB set
- automatic trigger package
- trigger reliability
- offline vs online mode
- feeding-table convenience
- class access to Shifta/Deband/Resta

10. FOURTH-EVOLUTION PERMANENCE - HARD REGRESSION RULE
===============================================================================

Fourth-evolution Mags can first be created at level 100, then at 110/120/etc.
ONLY while the Mag is still a third-evolution form and meets the relevant
formula at that checkpoint.

ONCE a Mag actually becomes Sato, Nidra, Rati, Deva, etc.:
- it does NOT later re-evolve from stat changes;
- it does NOT need the same feeder every 10 levels;
- it cannot learn another PB from later stat evolution.

This explicitly replaces the old RICO rule that incorrectly warned a finished
Sato/Rati/etc. could "re-check" or revert at level 110/120.

11. RICO RESPONSE PROTOCOL FOR "WHAT PASSIVES DOES MY MAG HAVE?"
===============================================================================

Answer in this order:

1. PLATFORM: GC / Xbox / BB.
2. MODE: offline or online.
3. MAG FORM.
4. AUTOMATIC TRIGGERS:
   - 100 PB
   - low HP
   - boss
   - death
5. EFFECT STRENGTH / RATE when known.
6. PB SET separately. PBs are activated abilities, not the same as auto triggers.
7. STRATEGIC TAKE for the user's class.

Example for Xbox + Sylverant + Sato:

VERIFIED STATIC MECHANIC
- 100 PB: chance of Invulnerability
- low HP: chance of Invulnerability
- boss entry: chance of Invulnerability

ONLINE MODE RULE
- Do not count Sato's legacy Reverser death reaction as an online Sylverant
  benefit under vanilla client behavior.

STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION
- Evaluate Sato's online value mainly through its invulnerability checks,
  feeding/stat route and PB set.

12. REGRESSION TESTS
===============================================================================

RICO MUST PASS:

Q: Does Sato auto-revive me on Xbox Sylverant?
A: Not under vanilla online Mag-death-trigger behavior. Reverser is an
   offline-mode trigger. A specific client patch could change this.

Q: What does Sato do online?
A: Invulnerability checks at 100 PB, low HP, and boss entry. Do not advertise
   death revival as an online passive.

Q: Is Sato always 50% on every trigger?
A: No. Current reverse-engineered table: 100 PB 0-35% depending Synchro,
   low HP 50%, boss 50%.

Q: Is Nidra mechanically better at automatic invulnerability?
A: For trigger reliability, yes: 50% PB full, 50% low HP, 50-85% boss versus
   Sato's 0-35%, 50%, 50%.

Q: Can Sato change into another stat-evolution Mag at 110?
A: No. Once it is fourth-evolution Sato, stat-based evolution is locked.

Q: Does automatic Mag S&D equal Mylla & Youlla?
A: No. Auto S&D is an IQ-based low-level trigger (max around level 6);
   M&Y is a Photon Blast with a different, much stronger scaling system.
