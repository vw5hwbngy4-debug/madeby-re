0. AUTHORITY AND SAFETY RULE
===============================================================================

A special-MAG statement is incomplete unless RICO knows BOTH:
1) the client/platform, and
2) when online/private-server content matters, the server.

Never silently convert:
- GameCube/Xbox stock-era knowledge into Ephinea knowledge;
- Ephinea/Blue Burst cell availability into GameCube/Xbox availability;
- one private server's custom quest/reward into another server's behavior.

For special MAG questions separate these four facts:

A. TRANSFORMATION REQUIREMENT
   Which Mag cell is used, on which Mag/form, and at which level/stat/ID conditions?

B. HISTORICAL STOCK CONSOLE ACQUISITION
   How was the cell/container obtained on Sega-era GameCube/Xbox online service?

C. CURRENT PRIVATE-SERVER ACQUISITION
   How is it obtainable now on Schtserv, Sylverant/Altimira, Ragol, etc.?

D. CURRENT AVAILABILITY
   Is the event/quest/reward active today, seasonal, permanent, server-exclusive,
   discontinued, or not established by this module?

If D is not established, say so instead of guessing.

1. ORIGINAL XBOX ONLINE IN 2026: SERVER IDENTITY MATTERS
===============================================================================

Original Xbox PSO Episode I & II can play online through Insignia.

At the time of this research, Insignia's PSO page offers these server choices:

RICO behavior:
- If an Xbox user asks "How do I get X online?", ask which server they selected
  if the answer is server-dependent.
- If the user says "Xbox with Insignia" but no server, do NOT assume Ephinea.
- Ephinea is a Blue Burst server and is not the Xbox server selected through Insignia.

Sylverant-specific current connection facts:
- Xbox PSO requires Insignia.
- To use Sylverant, select Sylverant as the PSO server in Insignia.
- Sylverant supports normal GameCube/Xbox crossplay for PSO Episode I & II.
- Guild Card exchange and Xbox Live voice features do not cross between Xbox and
  GameCube even though regular gameplay generally does.

3. HISTORICAL SEGA-ERA GAMECUBE/XBOX HOLIDAY ACQUISITION
===============================================================================

These routes describe Sega-era GameCube/Xbox online quest behavior and are useful
for identifying the original console design. They do NOT prove that a modern
private server currently offers the same route unchanged.

PIONEER HALLOWEEN
Platform documented historically: GameCube + Xbox
Players: 1-4

Core route:
- Complete Pioneer Halloween and obtain Cakes.
- Different players/characters can produce different cake combinations.
- Trade/collect 8 different Cakes.
- Give the 8 different Cakes to the hidden Rappy.
- Receive a Jack-O'-Lantern.
- Historical route: one Jack-O'-Lantern per character through this exchange.
- Opening the Jack-O'-Lantern gives a random classic Halloween MAG cell.

Historical/original-style Jack-O'-Lantern cell pool:
- Cell of Mag 502
- Cell of Mag 213
- Parts of RoboChao
- Heart of Opa Opa
- Heart of Pian
- Heart of Chao
- Heart of Chu Chu
- Heart of Kapu Kapu

Historical guides sometimes contain a typo "Cell of Mag 512".
Treat that as Cell of Mag 502.

Do NOT preserve old community estimates such as "Hallo Rappy drops it 95% I
believe" as an exact verified rate. The wording itself shows uncertainty.

PIONEER CHRISTMAS
Platform documented historically: GameCube + Xbox
Players: 1

Core route 1:
- Solve the Level 3 puzzle sequence without errors to unlock Level 4.
- Get a perfect result on Level 4.
- Receive a Christmas Present.
- Historical route: one such present per character.

Core route 2:
- Unlock/collect differently wrapped presents via the wrapping-paper system.
- Donate nine differently wrapped presents to the woman on Pioneer 2.
- Receive another Christmas Present.
- Historical route: one such donation reward per character.

Original-style Christmas Present classic cell pool:
- Heart of Angel
- Heart of Devil
- Panther's Spirit
- Kit of Mark III
- Kit of Master System
- Kit of Genesis
- Kit of Sega Saturn
- Kit of Dreamcast
- Kit of Hamburger

This is the historical console route that connects Christmas Present ->
Panther's Spirit -> Level 50+ Naga -> Panzer's Tail.

5. CURRENT SYLVERANT / ALTIMIRA GAMECUBE + XBOX CUSTOM CONTENT
===============================================================================

Sylverant supports GameCube/Xbox crossplay and the Altimira ship has extensive
updated, ported, and custom quest content.

ALTMIRA: CHRISTMAS CATASTROPHE
- Custom/Altimira quest.
- In Ultimate, a player can earn a Christmas Card under the quest's documented
  conditions.
- Three Christmas Cards can be traded to Phil near the Principal's Office for a
  Christmas Present.

IMPORTANT:
The fact that Altimira awards an item named "Christmas Present" does NOT, by
itself, prove that its current content table is identical to Sega-era,
Schtserv, or Ephinea Christmas Present tables.
For a precise current Sylverant Panther's Spirit acquisition claim, verify the
present's current Sylverant contents or current event documentation.

ALTMIRA: HALLOWEEN HORROR
- Custom/Altimira quest.
- Hidden switches unlock a secret route.
- On Ultimate, the final secret can lead to Dark Falz.
- Defeating Dark Falz awards Unknown Residue.
- Unknown Residue can be traded with the scientist on Pioneer 2 for a
  Jack-O'-Lantern.

Again, do not assume the container's contents are identical across servers
without server-specific confirmation.

ALTMIRA CUSTOM QUEST PRINCIPLE
Altimira demonstrates that a private console server can:
- port/modify Sega or Blue Burst quests;
- create new quests;
- create secret routes;
- award server-defined currencies/items;
- create bespoke acquisition paths for items.

Therefore "available on Xbox" is not a single timeless fact once private
servers are involved. Always bind availability to server + quest + date/event
when relevant.
