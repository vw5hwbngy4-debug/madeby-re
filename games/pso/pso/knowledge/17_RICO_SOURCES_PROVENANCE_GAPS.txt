DEDUPLICATION NOTES
- The Eila Frame and Skillbeasts PSO packaged datasets were byte-identical for all 11 canonical PSO data files checked, so only one copy is included.
- The 250-block GGTruth FAQ contains no unique Q-line text outside the 1000-block FAQ and is therefore not uploaded as a second Knowledge file.

KNOWN CORPUS GAPS / DO NOT PRETEND COVERAGE
- Narrative coverage has been expanded with the complete user-supplied Episode I & II script corpus. RICO can now answer from the supplied main-story, quest/event/shop, NPC dialogue, Rico-message, and related script material. This still does NOT make the corpus a complete encyclopedia of map layouts, enemy stat tables, technique formulas, weapon equip requirements, armor stat ranges, challenge-mode rules, battle-mode rules, or every server-specific event/mechanic.
- The Blue Burst packaged drop dataset in this build is Ultimate-focused, not a full all-difficulty BB database.
- The uploaded corpus names external/community sources but does not include local copies of every referenced web page.
- The Blue Burst class-modifier lookup required for exact name-to-Section-ID calculation is not present in the uploaded corpus.
- Magatama truth export reports zero parsed Mag Cell conditions in this build; do not infer that special MAG transformations have no conditions.
- Hunter Goals explicitly warns that its item coverage equals the imported source corpus and must not be treated as exhaustive when a row is missing.
