EMERALD v0.1.24.9.1.2 — FIELD SEARCH KEYBOARD GUARD

Fixes a Mobile Safari input leak in PokéField CALL → POKÉMON search.

Observed regression:
- the search box receives focus but typed virtual-keyboard keys are also seen by the emulator
- Emerald reacts to those keys, e.g. opening/navigating the in-game Pokédex
- search text can fail to enter normally

Fix:
- keyboard events are allowed to reach PokéField input/select/textarea editors normally
- at the PokéField picker boundary, keydown/keyup/keypress bubbling is stopped before the emulator's global keyboard listener
- the Pokémon search input also has a direct target-level propagation guard as defense-in-depth
- no preventDefault() is used, so Mobile Safari keeps its normal text-entry behavior
- applies to Pokémon search and numeric PokéField picker inputs

Cumulative base: v0.1.24.9.1.1 TEAM LAB STATE SYNC.
