EMERALD v0.1.24.8.5.1 — TIER FOCUS HANDOFF

Fixes the tier-list → FOCUS grammar.

Problem
- Vertical tier lists rendered correctly, but Pokémon cells and tier labels intercepted taps.
- A user could see SS/S/A/B/C but FOCUS remained 0 unless the thin empty band background itself was tapped.

Fix
- Every tier row is now a real selection surface.
- Tap the tier band, its label/count, or any Pokémon inside the tier.
- The selected tier becomes PRESENT.
- FOCUS immediately shows that exact population count and becomes armed.
- Selected tier band and member cells receive an amber highlight.
- FOCUS preserves the normal PokéField grammar: tier → PRESENT → FOCUS → CALL/refine → FOCUS again → SCATTER.

No changes to Save Vault, Transfer, Team Lab return toggle, My Party default-open, ADV STAB Axis, or True PokéField CALL semantics.
