EMERALD v0.1.21 — TEAM LAB FULL STACK
Date: 2026-09-09
Scope: additive read-only intelligence. No emulator/save writes.

1. PC BOXES
- Reads Emerald PokemonStorage through the BPEE runtime pointer, not a fixed EWRAM save-block address.
- PokemonStorage layout is currentBox @ +0x0000, boxes @ +0x0001, 14 boxes × 30 BoxPokemon × 80 bytes.
- One box is checksum-gated per live poll; cache converges over the 14 boxes without blocking the emulator.
- Boxed level is derived from stored experience + extracted Emerald growth-rate tables.
- Public state now contains state.boxes[], currentPcBox and pcBoxRevision.

2. REACTIVE MY FIELD
- Party + all decoded PC box specimens share one field.
- Known deterministic predicates apply immediately: species/final form/type, nature, ability, IV, EV total, Hidden Power type/power, Pokérus, ribbons, shiny, level, friendship, location/box, tier, base stat, 101-Sub capability.
- FOCUS is optional commitment. SCATTER resets.
- RANK X and RANK Y are immediate.
- DUAL RANK shows the same filtered population in two independent ordered tables.
- CROSS plots two metrics on X/Y axes.
- Clicking a boxed specimen opens that exact specimen in TeamLab.

3. THREAT LAB
- ADV OU + UUBL final species are a structural threat lens.
- Current party receives inspectable structural coverage/holes. This is explicitly a heuristic, not a battle proof.
- Select a threat, your move, foe move and field weather.

4. EXACT SUPPORTED RSE BENCHMARKS
- Gen III integer stat formulas with IV/EV/nature.
- Showdown-style fixed-point modifier rounding for supported RSE multipliers.
- Type-based Gen III physical/special categories.
- Fixed-power damage ranges with burn, weather, STAB, type effectiveness, Reflect/Light Screen hooks, supported abilities/items and Gen III random roll order.
- Seismic Toss/Night Shade/Dragon Rage/Sonic Boom fixed damage supported.
- Hidden Power uses the exact live specimen type + power.
- Variable-power/stateful moves are rejected instead of receiving fake precision.
- Speed benchmark uses explicit max-Speed Lv100 opponent assumptions.
- Damage benchmark uses an explicit 252 HP + 252 relevant-defense opponent.
- Survival benchmark uses an explicit max-offense opponent.

5. EV OPTIMIZER
- Computes minimum Speed EV to outspeed the selected benchmark.
- Computes minimum relevant attacking EV for guaranteed KO where reachable.
- Computes minimum HP + Def/SpD pair to survive the selected maximum roll where reachable.
- Merges those monotonic requirements into an exact minimum target from the current specimen.
- Also shows a clean-spread target assuming EV-reducing berries/reset.
- Never silently exceeds the 510 total; conflicts are surfaced.

6. THEME-CONSTRAINED SIX-STACK OPTIMIZER
Themes:
- Balance
- Rain
- Sun
- Baton Pass
- SubSeed
- Spikes / phaze

Scopes:
- OWNED ONLY: party + PC boxes, with specimen-readiness as a small tie/selection factor.
- ALL EMERALD: all non-Uber final species in the knowledge core.

Pins:
- selected specimen
- current party
- no pins

The optimizer uses beam search over theme fit, pair synergy, role diversity, shared weaknesses, STAB reach and ADV OU/UUBL structural coverage. When a theme-satisfying formation exists, unsatisfied formations are not returned ahead of it. The score is intentionally described as a structural heuristic, not solved competitive optimality.

7. PRESERVED v0.1.20 TEAMLAB
- Evolution routes
- future worthwhile STAB + levels
- TM/HM/tutor/egg acquisition
- competitive archetypes
- Smogon RS tactical labels where supplied/audited
- owned partner fit
- 101 HP Substitute breakpoint
- current six Team Fabric

SOURCE PRECEDENCE
- Emerald acquisition/species/growth data: supplied pret/pokeemerald extraction + exact BPEE lineage.
- Battle mechanics/tier context: supplied Pokemon Showdown Gen III lineage.
- Tactical prose: supplied audited Smogon ADV200 tactical atlas only.
- Partner/team/threat/optimizer scores: derived TeamLab heuristics, always labeled as derived.
