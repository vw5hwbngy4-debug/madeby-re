EMERALD v0.1.24.9.1.1 — TEAM LAB STATE SYNC

Fixes a Team Lab regression where LIVE could show the loaded party while Team Lab rendered the empty "Load Emerald" state.

Root cause / hardening:
- Team Lab previously relied on its cached `state` plus the `jrpg:emerald-state` event stream.
- If that cached reference was stale or the relevant event was missed during a view/focus transition, Team Lab could render an empty state while the canonical `globalThis.JRPGWorldEmeraldState` was already populated.

Fix:
- Team Lab now synchronizes from `globalThis.JRPGWorldEmeraldState` before reading owned specimens.
- It synchronizes again when Team Lab opens and before resolving the current specimen.
- Existing state events remain in place.
- No changes to True PokéField, Coverage, Focus Chain, Save Vault, Transfer, or Trainer Card mechanics.
