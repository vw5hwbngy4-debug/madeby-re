EMERALD v0.1.24.8.4 · ADV STAB AXIS

- Gen III type-based Physical/Special STAB evaluation.
- Best species STAB fit compares STAB-adjusted BP against the relevant base offensive stat.
- Accuracy plus major two-turn/recharge tempo costs are included; this remains a transparent heuristic, not a target-specific damage calc.
- Hidden Power is treated specially: species ceiling can surface a legal same-type Hidden Power 70 when TM10 is in the family movepool; exact specimens only use their actual IV-derived Hidden Power.
- Strategy snapshot replaces NEXT STAB with BEST STAB FIT.
- Team Lab adds GEN III STAB AXIS with Physical vs Special alternatives.
- True PokéField adds STAB AXIS CALL and BEST/PHYS/SPEC STAB tier ranks.
- Example sanity target: Salamence species ceiling = Hidden Power Flying 70 (Physical), ahead of Dragon Claw 80 (Special) on neutral raw pressure because ATK 135 > SP.ATK 110.
