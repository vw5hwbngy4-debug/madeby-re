PAL PARK BUFFER SEMANTICS NOW REPRODUCED
----------------------------------------
PK3 -> PK4 does not simply write clean UTF-16 names.
The v0.1.24 compiler now reproduces the PKHeX-modeled Pal Park behavior:

1. Start with the language-specific Pal Park nickname buffer residue.
2. Copy that residue into the PK4 nickname trash buffer beginning at byte +4.
3. Convert the original Gen III nickname glyphs into Gen IV glyph values and overlay only the used prefix.
4. Copy the first 16 bytes of the resulting nickname buffer into the OT trash buffer.
5. Convert and overlay the Gen III OT prefix into that inherited OT buffer.

Language-specific initial nickname trash used by the oracle:
EN (2): 18 20 0D 02 42 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 48 A1 0C 02 E0 FF
FR (3): 74 20 0D 02 42 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 A4 A1 0C 02 E0 FF
IT (4): 54 20 0D 02 42 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 84 A1 0C 02 E0 FF
DE (5): 74 20 0D 02 42 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 A4 A1 0C 02 E0 FF
ES (7): 74 20 0D 02 42 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 A4 A1 0C 02 E0 FF
JP (1): no initial trash residue in this code path.
