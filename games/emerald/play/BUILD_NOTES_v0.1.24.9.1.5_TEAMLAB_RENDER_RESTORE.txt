EMERALD v0.1.24.9.1.5 — TEAM LAB RENDER RESTORE
Date: 2026-09-12

ROOT CAUSE FOUND
----------------
The v0.1.24.9 Coverage Lens merge accidentally removed five Team Lab UI helper
functions while the Threat Lab renderer still called them:

- abilityFor
- moveOptions
- chooseThreat
- damageText
- evTiles

Because threatLab() is evaluated while the large Team Lab HTML template is being
constructed, the first missing helper throws a ReferenceError BEFORE section.innerHTML
is assigned. The mode button therefore becomes active and the Team Lab shell becomes
visible, but the panel itself remains an empty green/teal rounded surface. This exactly
matches the iPhone screenshots from 2026-09-12.

FIX
---
- Restored the five helper functions from the last pre-Coverage Team Lab lineage.
- Kept teamlab-engine-v0.1.24.9.js untouched so PokéField Coverage Lens remains current.
- Kept true-pokefield-v0.1.24.9.1.2.js untouched so Focus Chain + keyboard guard remain current.
- Kept the v0.1.24.9.1.4 race-proof Team Lab nav/state work.
- Added a render guard: future Team Lab render exceptions paint an explicit recovery
  message instead of silently leaving an empty panel.
- Updated the visible recent-build label to v0.1.24.9.1.5.

NO ROLLBACK
-----------
Coverage Lens, tier focus handoff, Focus Chain, Field search keyboard guard, ADV STAB
axis, CALL/FOCUS/SCATTER, DEX and Trainer Card remain on the latest merged lineage.
