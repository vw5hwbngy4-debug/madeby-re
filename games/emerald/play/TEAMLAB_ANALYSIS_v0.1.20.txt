TEAM LAB v0.1.20 — ANALYSIS / ARCHITECTURE
JRPG.WORLD · Pokémon Emerald Live
2026-09-09

GOAL
Turn the live Emerald specimen reader into a development + competitive relationship laboratory:
CURRENT SPECIMEN -> FUTURE EVOLUTION -> WORTHWHILE MOVES -> ACQUISITION -> ARCHETYPE -> PARTNERS -> SIX-POKEMON FABRIC.

SOURCE STACK
1. pret/pokeemerald supplied source
   Mechanical and Emerald acquisition truth: species, base stats, abilities, evolutions, level-up moves, TM/HM learnsets, tutors, egg moves, egg groups, EV yield and Gen III type chart.
2. supplied Pokemon Showdown source
   Gen III tier context and a later path to exact validation/simulation.
3. supplied audited STACKATAKA ADV200 Smogon Tactical Atlas
   Strategy provenance for 101 covered final forms. Kept separate from deterministic mechanics.
4. live Emerald state
   Exact specimen truth: level, IVs, EVs, nature, ability, Hidden Power, current moves, Pokérus, friendship and party membership.

EXTRACTED KNOWLEDGE CORE
- 386 species
- 355 moves
- 214 terminal/final species identities in the complete Gen I-III evolution graph
- 101 species with attached audited tactical-atlas entries
- full evolution branching, including Nincada -> Ninjask / Shedinja
- complete family move access retained separately from the 40-move competitive display shortlist
- direct acquisition provenance attached per competitive move

WHY FULL FAMILY MOVE ACCESS MATTERS
A display shortlist cannot safely drive mechanics. TeamLab therefore keeps a complete family move list for deterministic capability checks. A move omitted from the visible top-40 list can still enable a role or relationship.

DEVELOPMENT MODEL
For a live specimen, TeamLab resolves all reachable final branches. A default branch is selected deterministically but branch choices remain visible.
The STAB roadmap prefers:
1. naturally reachable level-up acquisition along the normal evolution path;
2. TM/HM;
3. tutor;
4. Move Reminder / early-stage timing;
5. delayed-evolution routes;
6. breeding a new specimen.
This avoids misleading cases such as displaying Sceptile's Lv29 Leaf Blade as if a normally evolved Sceptile could naturally exist at Lv29. The natural route is Grovyle Lv29.

COMPETITIVE MOVE MODEL
Move value is an inspectable heuristic, not canonical viability.
Inputs include Gen III move type/category, base power, accuracy, STAB and the projected final species' relevant attacking stat.
High-value utility moves such as Substitute, Leech Seed, Baton Pass, Spore, Spikes, Rapid Spin, Recover and setup moves are explicitly surfaced.
Hidden Power is special-cased from the live specimen because its exact type and base power are IV-derived. The Gen III type-based physical/special split is applied to that exact type.

ARCHETYPE ENABLEMENT
Archetypes are generated from legal move/ability combinations, for example:
- SubSeed
- SubPunch
- Baton Pass + transferable setup
- Dragon Dance
- Calm Mind
- Swords Dance
- Bulk Up
- Belly Drum
- Rain / Swift Swim
- Sun / Chlorophyll / Solar Beam
- Spikes
- Rapid Spin
- RestTalk
- status pressure
- fast physical/special offense
- physical/special tank profiles
Where the supplied audited atlas has a species entry, its Smogon RS tactical route labels and tactical summary are shown separately as sourced strategy.

ACQUISITION ROUTING
HOW TO BUILD IT exposes the best route found in exact Emerald data:
- level + species stage
- TM/HM number
- one-time tutor + location
- Battle Frontier tutor
- egg route + root species + direct compatible fathers that can carry the move by level-up
Direct father suggestions are compatibility routes, not local-catchability claims. Trades, version exclusivity and multi-hop breeding chains remain a later router layer.

PAIR FIT
Pair Fit is relational rather than a global tier score. Channels include:
- defensive weakness/resistance complement
- shared weaknesses
- STAB target expansion
- physical/special offense complement
- bulk complement
- Baton Pass recipient value
- Rain/Sun compatibility
- SubSeed pressure + bulk/hazard complement
- role overlap penalties
The UI exposes reasons and risks rather than only a scalar.

PARTNER LENSES
- OWNED FIT: exact specimens currently in the party
- STRUCTURAL CANDIDATES: mechanics-first non-Uber terminal species
- ADV COMPETITIVE LENS: OU / UUBL candidates ranked by the same relational model
This makes questions such as "who benefits from Ninjask's Speed/Attack pass?" and "who structurally complements Sceptile?" visible without pretending tier alone is synergy.

TEAM FABRIC
The current party is projected to its selected final forms and analyzed as one stack:
- 17-type defensive matrix
- weakness/resistance/immunity counts
- shared-pressure detection
- offensive STAB super-effective reach over all terminal species
- capability roles such as physical/special pressure, bulk, Speed plan, weather, hazards, removal, phazing, recovery, status and Baton Pass
- coherent lines such as Rain core, Spikes + phazing and Baton Pass
- structural gaps
- next-slot candidate deltas while fewer than six slots are occupied

101 HP SUBSTITUTE BREAKPOINT
Only shown when the projected final species can access Substitute.
Correct Gen III Lv100 formula:
HP = 2*Base + IV + floor(EV/4) + 110.
404 HP creates a 101 HP Substitute, which survives one Lv100 Seismic Toss.
The initial prototype incorrectly used +210; v0.1.20 corrects this and has regression tests.

WHAT THIS VERSION DOES NOT CLAIM
- It is not yet a full six-slot optimizer.
- It does not calculate exact damage benchmarks against configured foe sets yet.
- It does not optimize EVs against speed / OHKO / survival benchmarks yet.
- It does not yet track whether a one-use TM/tutor has already been consumed in this save.
- It does not yet prove whether an egg father is catchable on this cartridge without trading.
- My Field still receives the runtime-owned party projection, not all PC boxes. TeamLab is written against the published state so box specimens can be added later without changing its knowledge core.
- Candidate rankings are derived structural hypotheses. Mechanical facts remain separate and source-grounded.

NEXT LOGICAL TEAMLAB LAYERS
A. Reactive My Field over party + boxes: deterministic filters apply instantly, CALL reserved for composite reasoning.
B. Threat Lab: current team defensive/offensive matchup matrix versus an ADV OU threat pool.
C. Benchmark Lab: exact Speed, damage and survival thresholds using Showdown mechanics.
D. EV Optimizer: minimum EV investment to hit selected speed / damage / survival / 101-Sub / berry thresholds.
E. Theme-constrained Team Optimizer: Rain, Sun, Baton Pass, SubSeed, etc. while retaining theme identity and repairing OU threat holes.
F. Full six-stack search: candidate additions re-scored after every slot because pairwise goodness does not guarantee team coherence.
