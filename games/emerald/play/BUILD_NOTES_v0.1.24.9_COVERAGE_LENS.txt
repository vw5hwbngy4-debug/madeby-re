EMERALD v0.1.24.9 · COVERAGE LENS
==================================
Date: 2026-09-12
Base: v0.1.24.8.6 APP CALM · SINGLE SURFACE

WHY THIS BUILD
--------------
Coverage already existed in fragments before v0.1.24.9:
- Team Lab had shared-weakness counts and a collapsed defensive type matrix.
- Team Lab had structural coverage / STAB-reach heuristics.
- The STAB-axis work understood Gen III physical/special type categories.

But the old coverage path was fragmented and mostly type-only:
- ability immunities/resistance modifiers were not consistently applied to team weakness summaries;
- Field could select/rank populations, but could not analyze the current population as a coverage surface;
- the DEX did not expose per-species weakness/resistance/immunity context;
- offensive coverage was not clearly separated into typing-only vs exact current moves vs legal movepool.

v0.1.24.9 extends the existing engine instead of creating a separate type-chart page.

POKÉFIELD COVERAGE LENS
-----------------------
- Adds one-tap COVERAGE quick access beside TIER.
- Coverage always analyzes the current Field population:
  ALL 386, current CALL responders, current FOCUS, or a selected tier population.
- DEFENSE lens paints the current population by one incoming type:
  severe weakness / weakness / resistance / immunity / neutral.
- DEFENSE is ability-aware where the ability is exact or deterministic.
- OFFENSE lens supports:
  * TYPING: STAB-type reach only.
  * CURRENT MOVES when every selected member maps to an exact move-known specimen.
  * LEGAL MOVEPOOL otherwise, including Hidden Power potential/exact Hidden Power where known.
- Coverage subsets hand directly into existing FOCUS grammar:
  WEAK / SAFE / IMMUNE or SUPER EFFECTIVE / NEUTRAL+ -> PRESENT -> FOCUS.
- CALL / FOCUS / SCATTER / TIER remain intact; Coverage is a lens on the same population, not a new database page.
- Species detail now includes Weak / Resists / Immune before STAB notes.

ABILITY-AWARE DEFENSE
---------------------
Implemented in the shared TeamLab engine:
- Levitate: Ground immunity
- Flash Fire: Fire immunity
- Volt Absorb: Electric immunity
- Water Absorb: Water immunity
- Wonder Guard: blocks non-super-effective damaging types in this coverage model
- Thick Fat: halves Fire/Ice effectiveness again

When a specimen has an exact ability, that exact ability is used.
When species data exposes exactly one unique deterministic ability, that ability may be used at species level.
Ambiguous multi-ability species are not silently assigned one ability.

DEX COVERAGE
------------
- DEX live snapshot now includes COVERAGE · DEFENSE for the current species.
- Uses the current species typing, not the projected evolution's typing.
- Shows weaknesses with multipliers, plus resistances and immunities.
- Ability-based immunities include the ability name where available.

TEAM LAB COVERAGE
-----------------
- Existing TEAM FABRIC is retained and upgraded rather than replaced.
- Shared pressure / defensive rows are ability-aware.
- STAB SE REACH label becomes MOVE SE REACH because the engine now includes move coverage.
- Shows super-effective reach and neutral+ reach.
- Adds concise LIVE DEFENSIVE PRESSURE for risky types.
- Existing full matrix remains available as a collapsed, ability-aware party matrix.
- OPEN COVERAGE IN FIELD hands the party analysis into the Field surface.

TRUTH / SCOPE BOUNDARIES
------------------------
- This is not a full battle simulator and does not claim damage proof from coverage alone.
- TYPING mode describes type/STAB reach, not a specific four-move set.
- CURRENT MOVES is only used when exact move-known selected specimens are available.
- LEGAL MOVEPOOL describes possible legal move coverage from supplied Emerald/ADV data.
- Full 'remove one teammate and show live delta / single-point-of-failure' visualization is not included in this build yet.
