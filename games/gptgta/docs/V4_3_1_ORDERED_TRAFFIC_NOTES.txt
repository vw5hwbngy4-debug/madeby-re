GPTGTA v4.3.1 — Ordered Traffic / Big City Harbor

This build keeps the v4.3 large city and Harbor Scrap objective, but changes the traffic model.

Changes:
- Traffic cars now spawn onto soft lane centers instead of random road pixels.
- Cars follow ordered loops through the city grid.
- Traffic uses simple two-phase stoplights at intersections.
- Cars keep spacing and wait behind cars ahead.
- Ambient traffic no longer destroys itself through normal traffic-to-traffic contact.
- Carjacker ambient hijacks are much rarer.
- The player can still crash into traffic, steal cars with drivers, and cash cars at Harbor Scrap.

Design goal:
The city should feel alive and structured, more like GTA 1/2 traffic: many cars, loops, rules, occasional chaos.
