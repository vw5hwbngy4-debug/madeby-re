Ash District v0.10 — Tower Road / Dialogue Camp Pass

Changed:
- Removed playable/drivable car context. Vehicles are only abandoned scenery.
- Replaced the grid-like forest feel with a smoother green wilds background and a long dirt road from Ember Camp to Skyspine Tower.
- Skyspine Tower is now the travel object/corridor to the city environment.
- Vendors/persons in camp no longer open buy/sell grids; they provide short dialogue only. Stash remains interactive.
- Zombie roaming avoids the town edge more aggressively, especially while the player is near the gates.

Known limitations:
- Tower corridor is represented as a transition object, not a full indoor dungeon yet.
- Inventory grid is unchanged from v0.8/v0.9; future pass should simplify mobile use.
- Cars still appear as static obstacles/scenery.
