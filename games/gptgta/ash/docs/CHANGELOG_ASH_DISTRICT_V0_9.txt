Ash District v0.9 — Camp Roads / Context / Vehicle Pass

Changed:
- Added context interaction UI for camp NPCs, stash, travel passages and vehicles.
- E now performs the nearest context action; a visible context button appears when relevant.
- Mobile gets a MOL button for quick Molotov throws.
- Q throws the equipped Molotov/throwable without changing weapon loadout.
- Monsters now spawn and roam farther from safe towns; when the player is in town they no longer wait directly at the fence edge.
- Ember Camp now renders as a barricaded asphalt town instead of a green square.
- Outside field now reads more like green wilds with dirt roads.
- Added an Apartment Passage near the forest edge leading to a small City Outpost safe town.
- Added basic drivable vehicles outside town; enter with E near a car, exit with E, and ram zombies by driving into them.
- City Outpost has stash/vendor context and safe-zone behavior like Ember Camp.

Known limitations:
- Vehicles use simple top-down movement, not full car physics.
- The Apartment Passage is a simple area transition, not a full indoor dungeon yet.
- Inventory is still one-item-per-square; no true multi-tile Diablo Tetris grid yet.
- Vendors still use compact grid trading rather than full dialogue tabs.
