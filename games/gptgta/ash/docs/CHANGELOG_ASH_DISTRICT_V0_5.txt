Ash District v0.5 — Ember Camp Town Pass

Changed the former green safe-zone into a first-town structure:
- Larger walkable safe town named Ember Camp.
- Barricaded perimeter with north/south gates.
- Campfire, tents, stash chest, healer, armorer and supplier NPCs.
- Grove/forest objects around the camp so the town sits between wilderness and city.
- Zombies remain outside the camp boundary.
- Wave progression waits while the player is inside town, then begins after leaving.
- Stash access is restricted to Ember Camp.
- Added E key camp supplies purchase: bullets, shells and healing for $35.
- Updated HUD, hint text and README.

Known limitations:
- Vendors are functional only through the simple E-key supply purchase for now.
- Camp objects are mostly visual; town collision remains open/walkable by design.
- No full quest/NPC dialogue system yet.
