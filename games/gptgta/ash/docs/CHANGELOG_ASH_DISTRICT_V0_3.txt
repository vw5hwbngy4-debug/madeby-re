Ash District v0.3 — Diablo-like polish pass

Changed files:
- public_html/index.html
- public_html/zombies/index.html
- public_html/ash-district.js
- public_html/ash-district.css
- public_html/README.txt
- public_html/docs/CHANGELOG_ASH_DISTRICT_V0_3.txt

Changes:
- Added world camera scaling: desktop now renders at 0.72 scale, mobile at 0.66 scale, showing more of the city.
- Corrected mouse/world targeting for the new zoom level.
- Added Safari/iOS anti-zoom guards: gesturestart/gesturechange/gestureend prevention, double-tap prevention, and controlled touchmove prevention outside inventory.
- Slowed zombie speeds substantially:
  - Walker: 62 -> 34
  - Runner: 108 -> 58
  - Brute: 48 -> 26
  - Spitter: 54 -> 30
  - Boss base: 42 -> 22
- Reduced zombie wave speed scaling from 1.8% per wave to 0.6% per wave.
- Added stamina system for sprinting.
- Added red life orb, blue level/XP orb, and center stamina bar.
- Added item power scoring and comparison against equipped item.
- Added affix/suffix generator for equipment names and stats.
- Added visible affix lines in inventory and equipment slots.
- Added special modifiers: burn chance, bleed chance, knockback, pickup radius, crit, fire rate, movement, armor, max HP, damage.

Known limitations:
- The blue orb currently represents level/XP rather than a full mana system.
- Buildings remain exterior obstacles; interior loot rooms are not implemented in this pass.
- Safari zoom prevention is best-effort; iOS Safari behavior can vary by browser settings and embedding context.
