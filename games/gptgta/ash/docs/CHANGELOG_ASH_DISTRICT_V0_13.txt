Ash District v0.13 Firepath Stash Pass

- Based on stable v0.11 firepath-road line.
- Fixed duplicate audio button declaration that could hard-crash boot.
- Mobile BAG button remains removed; PATH/Firepath is the skill button.

- Bag and stash slots reduced to 35 each so the mobile grid fits cleanly.
